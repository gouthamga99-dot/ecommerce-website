## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end consumer technology commerce through clinical precision, absolute optical clarity, and quiet digital luxury. Built for discerning technologists and design-conscious hardware buyers, the interface functions as a pristine gallery where products take primary focus.

The aesthetic fuses modern minimalism with industrial precision:
- **Atmospheric Clarity**: Predominantly white and frosted surfaces prioritize device photography and specifications without visual clutter.
- **Architectural Grounding**: Deliberate hairline gridlines, disciplined typographic hierarchy, and tactile Micro-elevations emulate premium hardware materials (anodized aluminum, polished ceramic, and sapphire crystal).
- **Engineered Momentum**: Kinetic feedback is sharp and damped, favoring subtle micro-interactions over expressive ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette establishes high dynamic range using deep slate typography against layered, pure light surfaces, accented by optical royal blues and electric cyan.

### Palette Mechanics
- **Base Canvas & Layering**:
  - `#FFFFFF`: Primary card surfaces, popovers, and elevated modal panels.
  - `#F8FAFC`: Global application background, providing subtle contrast against pure white cards.
  - `#F1F5F9`: Recessed structural containers, cart trays, and spec comparison blocks.
- **Borders & Dividers**:
  - `#E2E8F0`: Default surface perimeter lines, card borders, and persistent structural gutters.
  - `#CBD5E1`: Interactive field borders and active hover boundaries.
- **Typography & Content**:
  - `#0F172A` (Slate 900): Primary headlines, model designations, and prices.
  - `#334155` (Slate 700): Body specifications, long-form editorial copy, and primary inputs.
  - `#64748B` (Slate 500): Secondary metadata, inactive states, table labels, and helper text.
- **Accents & Telemetry**:
  - `#2563EB` (Primary): Buy actions, active filters, and primary links.
  - `#1D4ED8` (Primary Hover): Depressed/hover interaction states.
  - `#3B82F6` (Primary Focus): Focus rings and active indicator pills.
  - `#0284C7` & `#0EA5E9` (Secondary / Cyan Highlights): Key technical differentiators, badge highlights, live stock indicators, and feature tags.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Hanken Grotesk** for display, headings, and editorial content to project a sharp, contemporary look with neutral geometric proportions. 

Technical specs, SKU tags, system status indicators, currency amounts, and data values use **Geist** to supply engineering-grade legibility and structured rhythm.

All display and hero headings use strict negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic physical industrial branding. Body copy maintains neutral tracking for maximum readability across specs, configuration tables, and order reviews.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptable 12-column grid anchored by a maximum desktop width of `1440px`.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - **Desktop** (`>= 1024px`): 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Section vertical spacing adheres to `4rem` to `6rem` rhythm.
  - **Tablet** (`768px - 1023px`): 8 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1.5rem` (`24px`) margins.
  - **Mobile** (`<= 767px`): 4 columns, `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters, `1rem` (`16px`) margins. Product configurator panels pin to viewport bottom as fixed drawers.
- **Rhythm**: Component spacing strictly adheres to an 8px base grid, using `0.25rem` (`4px`) only for metadata tags and icon offsets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows an Apple-inspired ambient illumination model, prioritizing diffused multi-layer shadows and hair-thin borders over opaque stacking.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Base Level (Canvas)**: Background `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Grids)**: `#FFFFFF` filled cards enclosed with a `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` hairline border and ambient shadow:
  - `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover States)**: Active cards, catalog grid hover states, and dropdown menus:
  - `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
  - Border transitions to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Quick-View & Slide-out Cart)**: High-altitude overlays:
  - `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
  - Backdrops use `#0F172A` at `20%` opacity paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Frosted Header Layer**: The main navigation uses `#FFFFFF` at `85%` opacity, `1px` border-bottom `#E2E8F0`, and `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` for smooth scrolling over product imagery.

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on disciplined roundings that mirror modern tech device enclosures:

- **Component Standard (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Form inputs, standard buttons, badge containers, spec blocks, and product thumbnail cells.
- **Large Container (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Product feature cards, device comparison panels, and catalog tiles.
- **Extra Large Containers (`1.5rem` / `24px`)**: Hero product cards, checkout overview modules, and floating quick-drawers.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Category tags, inventory status pills, color swatch rings, and micro-steppers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#2563EB` fill, white `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` corner radius, `label-lg` font. Height: `44px` (desktop), `48px` (mobile). Hover: `#1D4ED8`. Active: `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary**: `#FFFFFF` background with `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background, `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background, `#334155` text. Hover: `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` text.
- **Tactile Details**: Focus states show an outer ring of `2px` `#FFFFFF` + `2px` `#3B82F6`.

### Cards & Product Showcase
- **Product Card**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface, `1px` `#E2E8F0` border, `1rem` corner radius. Contains high-resolution asset staged on `#F8FAFC` inner media box with `0.5rem` rounding. Subtle hover transforms lift the card `-2px` with a Tier 2 shadow.
- **Spec / Feature Card**: `#F8FAFC` background, `1px` `#E2E8F0` border, `0.75rem` padding, showcasing hardware metrics using `Geist` bold numbers and `Slate 500` labels.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Inputs**: Height `44px`, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` `#E2E8F0` border, `0.5rem` radius. Text is `#0F172A` with `#64748B` placeholder.
- **Focus State**: `1px` solid `#2563EB` with a `3px` aura of `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges**: Low-saturation tint backgrounds.
  - *New Arrival*: Background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)`, border `1px` solid `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`, text `#2563EB`.
  - *In Stock / High Performance*: Background `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`, border `1px` solid `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.2)`, text `#0284C7`.
- **Variant Selector Chips**: `#FFFFFF` container with `1px` `#E2E8F0` border. Active selected state uses `#0F172A` border (`2px`) with `#0F172A` text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes**: `18px` square, `4px` radius. Checked: `#2563EB` fill with crisp white vector checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: `18px` circular, unselected `1px` `#CBD5E1` border. Selected: `5px` inner dot `#2563EB` centered inside white boundary and `#2563EB` outer ring.

### Specialized Tech Commerce Components
- **Colorway Pickers**: `24px` circular swatches with a `2px` inset gap separating color fill from the surrounding selection indicator ring.
- **Storage/Spec Segments**: Multi-segment horizontal tabs in `#F1F5F9` track. Active item slides a white `#FFFFFF` card with soft Tier 1 shadow underneath the active choice.
- **Live Cart Flyout**: Pinned right viewport drawer, clean divider lines (`#E2E8F0`), real-time shipping progress bar with vibrant `#0284C7` accent fill.
